01

What a lesson looks like

Sixty minutes, one-on-one. Walk in if a coach is free. Clubs are here if you didn’t bring yours.

  1. A

    Baseline on the FlightScope

    Ball speed, launch, spin, carry—shot by shot. We agree on what the data says before we change anything.

  2. B

    Video from more than one angle

    Face-on and down-the-line next to the numbers. You see what I see. Then we pick one change that matters.

  3. C

    Putting gets its own sensor

    Capto reads face, path, and tempo in a focused thirty minutes. Useful when the green is where the card quietly falls apart.

02

Who you’ll work with

Two coaches under one roof. Instruction and fitting in the same bay.

Yaser Garcia

Head coach & founder

  • PGA of America Member
  • PGA PGM
  • ADM certified
  • TPI Level 2
  • Capto Putting certified

“No two golfers are the same. I work closely with you to understand your strengths and areas for improvement, and develop a coaching plan tailored to your unique needs and goals.” Yaser’s methodology covers all three pillars: physical conditioning, mental preparation, and technical skills training working together as one system. With 13+ years playing competitive golf, he dedicated his career to sharing the game—bringing FlightScope launch-monitor work, multi-angle video, Capto putting mastery, TPI fitness, and club fitting under one roof. He also serves as El Paso tour director for U.S. Kids Golf.

Memo

Memo Vega

Instructor & club fitting specialist

  • Private and junior instruction
  • Driver, iron, and full-bag fittings
  • Matches shafts and heads to your FlightScope data

“I pride myself on providing a personalized approach to each of my students — understanding the cause-and-effect relationship between actions and outcomes on the course.” Memo’s teaching philosophy is rooted in the mental side of the game. He uses communication and visualization techniques to make improvement stick between sessions, not just on the lesson mat. His patient, step-by-step approach has produced measurable results for everyone from beginners to competitive high school athletes.
