Perfect Swing 4U Golf Academy is an indoor, climate-controlled studio for instruction, fitting, and equipment—serving beginners through advanced players, including juniors, high schoolers, women, and military and veterans near Fort Bliss.

What a lesson looks like

You hit. We measure. Then we coach.

Sessions run one-on-one. Walk-ins are welcome when a coach is free, and we have clubs if you don’t bring your own.

  1. 01

    Baseline on the FlightScope

    Ball speed, launch angle, spin, and carry—captured shot by shot so the first conversation is about your ball flight, not a generic checklist.

  2. 02

    See the swing from more than one angle

    Multi-angle video sits beside the data. You and your coach look at the same frame, agree on the change that matters most, and leave with drills that match it.

  3. 03

    Putters get their own sensor

    Capto putting analysis reads face angle, path, and tempo in thirty focused minutes—useful when the green is where the scorecard quietly unravels.

Tools we use, in plain words

  • FlightScope launch monitor Numbers for every full swing—so progress isn’t a feeling you argue with later.
  • Multi-angle video Face-on and down-the-line views that make the coach’s notes stick.
  • Capto putting Stroke details you can’t see from standing over the ball alone.
  • TPI-based fitness Movement screens that keep the swing plan honest about your body.

“No two golfers are the same. I work closely with you to understand your strengths and areas for improvement, and develop a coaching plan tailored to your unique needs and goals.” Yaser’s methodology covers all three pillars: physical conditioning, mental preparation, and technical skills training working together as one system. With 13+ years playing competitive golf, he dedicated his career to sharing the game—bringing swing mechanics, Capto putting mastery, TPI fitness, and club fitting expertise under one roof.

Memo

Instructor & club fitting specialist

“I pride myself on providing a personalized approach to each of my students — understanding the cause-and-effect relationship between actions and outcomes on the course.” Memo’s teaching philosophy is rooted in the mental side of the game.
